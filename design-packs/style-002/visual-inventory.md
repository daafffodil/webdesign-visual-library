## Card families
Do not unify these under one generic Card: process illustration panels, chart evidence panels, integration icon tiles, gray workflow cards, seam-separated micro descriptions, image/text rows, feature-wall cells, elevated gradient focus cards, UI windows and small overlay dialogs all have different geometry.

## Section transitions
White remains the continuous page surface. Thin 1px seams and white masks soften panel boundaries; whitespace and typography signal sections. Most sections have no colored background band. The bottom CTA is the dominant large saturated surface. Product screenshots are repeatedly cropped and faded, not enclosed in ordinary marketing cards.

## Special behavior audit
Observed: full-width navigation overlay, full-screen mobile menu, preview selection and autoplay, badge icon replacement, check bounce, gradient button ring, logo hover, feature-card hover, side-masked rocking rows, native video controls. No confirmed sticky nav, horizontally draggable section, infinite marquee, whole-page stagger, parallax, or scroll-triggered blur/scale reveal. Fixed overlays in product pictures are illustrations, not actual fixed page elements.
